Privacy policy

Signalbox is used only by its owner, on the owner's own account.

  • It accesses Google data (mail and calendar) only after the owner signs in and grants consent, and only to show that data back to the owner.
  • Data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings under third-party connections.

Signalbox's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
